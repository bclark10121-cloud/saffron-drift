Saffron Drift

Ride the quiet desert

Ride, jump and wander.

Ride a magic carpet across a small, quiet desert basin full of ruins and old scrap. Climb old ruins, glide down from the top, and take the Drift: at sixteen, Tavi rides grandmother's faded carpet out to five keepers, each of whom weaves one pattern piece into it. Nothing can hurt you and the carpet always flies to you when you call. An early test: the world is simple shapes while the riding and climbing get tuned.

The story

Every child of the Hearth Caravan takes the Drift at sixteen: a solo journey across the Ochre Basin on the family carpet. Tavi's is grandmother's old carpet, faded to the color of sand. Mira, Rana, Kip, Pell and Sef each weave one piece into it: the Step, the Sun, the Spiral, the Water and the Star. Bring it home with all five and Elder Suna helps you make the pattern your own.

What kind of game is this?

The ride is the reward. A magic carpet that floats over the sand, drifts in turns, boosts in a trail of sand and leaves the ground over every crest.
Climb anything steep. Rock faces, the Leaning Tower and the Buried Ship. Push in or jump to grab on, hold still to rest, and ride warm updrafts back into the sky.
No way to lose. No enemies and no damage. A fall opens a glide bubble that floats you down.
Your journey, your pace. Five pattern pieces to earn, seven requests, scrap for Kip's upgrades and trails of gold coins for new dyes and boost trail colors.

Things to do

  • Find the three parts of your carpet around Home Camp and ride out.
  • Find five places and travel back to any of them from the Map.
  • Help seven people: carry water and seeds, ride the Old Road, wake the beacon, find a lost flute, climb a ship's mast and light the Old Fires on the Dry Falls lip.
  • Earn a pattern piece from each of the five keepers, then bring the carpet home and make the pattern your own.
  • Find six tassel charms. Each one hangs a gold tassel on your carpet and makes climbing last longer. One only a glide can reach.
  • Find scrap on the ruins and dunes, and upgrade your carpet and gliding stone with Kip at Home Camp.
  • Follow the coin trails and buy carpet dyes and boost trail colors from Rana at the Nomad Camp.
  • Choose who you will be, then keep riding.

Controls

Keyboard
WASD walks and climbs, or steers and goes on the carpet. Space jumps or lets go. E talks. Q gets on or off the carpet, or calls it. Shift boosts. Arrows or the mouse turn the camera. R puts the camera behind you. Esc opens the Menu.
Xbox controller
Left stick walks, climbs or steers. Right stick turns the camera. Click it to put the camera behind you. RT goes and LT brakes. A jumps or lets go. X talks. B gets on or off the carpet, or calls it. LB boosts. Menu or View opens the Menu.
Touch
Drag on the left side to walk and climb. On the carpet, drag up to go and sideways to steer. Drag on the right side to look. Act, Jump, Bike and Boost buttons on the right. Settings has Auto accelerate and No climbing limit.

Play with an Xbox controller

Any Xbox Wireless Controller with Bluetooth works. That is every Series X|S controller and the Xbox One controllers made since 2016. If the plastic around the Xbox button matches the face of the controller, it has Bluetooth. Other controllers that your phone or browser sees as a standard gamepad use the same buttons.

On Android
  1. Hold the Xbox button to turn the controller on.
  2. Hold the small Pair button on top for 3 seconds, until the Xbox button flashes fast.
  3. On your phone, open Settings, then Connected devices, then Pair new device.
  4. Tap Xbox Wireless Controller. The Xbox button stays lit when it is connected.
  5. Open Saffron Drift. The touch buttons hide as soon as you use the controller.
In your browser
  1. Connect the controller to your computer with a USB cable or Bluetooth.
  2. Open Play in your browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.
  3. Press any button once after the game loads, so the browser sees the controller.
Menus
The D-pad or left stick moves between buttons. A chooses and B goes back. You can play the whole game with only the controller.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android asks, allow installs from this source.
  4. Tap Install, then open the game.

Android may show a Play Protect notice for apps from outside the Play Store. Version 0.9.0 installs over the last version and keeps your saves.
